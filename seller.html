How to Add Images

We use free external hosting for fast loading. Follow these steps:

  1. Go to ImgBB.com.
  2. Click Start Uploading and select your product picture.
  3. Click the green Upload button.
  4. Change the dropdown below the image to Direct HTML link.
  5. Copy the image link (ending in .jpg or .png).
  6. Paste that link in the "Image URL" field here.
